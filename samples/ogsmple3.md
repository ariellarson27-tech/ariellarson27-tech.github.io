Midnight Magic
2008

We had spent the last hour before I was supposed to meet with Glenda watching the elves and fey practice with their weapon of choice, I paid close enough attention to realize that they had put some sort of shields on the blades so the worst damage that could be inflicted would be breaking a bone and severe bruising. Calum had found another glyph on the back of my neck and I was once again in a sour mood when she came up to us.

“What's wrong now?” She asked me.

“There's another glyph on the back of my neck.” I grumbled as I got off the grass to stand in front of her.

She frowned, “Well you're going to have to forget about them for now, we're going to try finding out your element and that means total focus.”

I completely ignored the glyphs and looked at her eagerly, “I'm ready to try.” I said in a calm voice. She nodded and lead me toward an empty glass dome, she walked right in with no trouble. I followed her and started feeling intense jolts of pain in the five places the glyphs were at. I flinched but kept going, she stopped in the middle of it.

“Tell me Marina, what do you know about basic elements?” She asked, sitting with her legs crossed and gesturing for me to do the same.

“One can defeat another but there are only so many ways, like air will strengthen a flame but water will put it out. I only know four elements though and those are the ones I practiced for three years.” I answered. “Earth, water, fire, and air.” I listed them off. “And you can combined different elements to create new ones. Fire and water can make mist and fog while air and water can make ice.”

She was clearly impressed by my knowledge, “Very good, but there is a few other elements you need to be aware of. Do you remember the black color the Dark Prince's magic takes?” I nodded, “That element is Dark, but I guess you should have figured that one out already. But these elements you don't know of have a different cycle for one cannot exist without the other.”

“So like Yin and Yang?” I asked.

She nodded, “Very much so.”

She let me think it over, “So Christopher's opposite would be Calum and his family, because their element is  Light?”

She smiled, “Very quick minded.” She commented, “Now to tell you of the rarer of the pairs that haven't had masters for the last three hundred years. Are you aware of what the wiccans in the mortal world call Midnight Magic or the Goddesses' Daughter?”

“Midnight Magic is done at midnight during certain phases of the moon. And the Goddess's Daughter is magic done with aide from the moon and usually by a female.” I said.

“So what would be the Gods' Son?” She pushed.

“Magic done with the aide of the sun and usually by a male.” I answered.

“That pair is called Day and Night. Christopher's ancestors destroyed the last of the Night Court ten years after I was born, I had met a young girl from there and she looks a lot like you.” She added thoughtfully.

“What happened to the Day court?” I asked after I realized she had tuned out as she thought of her memories, her eyes focused on me again.

“They disappeared, the last of them fled into the mortal world never to be seen again. She was pregnant at the time, I wonder if the babe survived... Anyway, when the queen told me the color of your magic I thought of the possibilities and you might just be a night fey born in a glamor or a dark fey that hasn't realized your own race yet.”

I balled my hand into fists, “I am _not_ from his court.” I said to no one in particular.

“I believe you, if you were you would have woken up to his touch and his marks. And you wouldn't have been able to come in here.” She said smoothly.
