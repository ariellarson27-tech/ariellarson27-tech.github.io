Eragon by Christopher Paolini
Fan-Fiction 2015

Morning came and we made our way out of the cave with the cover of fog and low hanging clouds into the frigid air near the tops of the mountains, I bent the air around me to be breathable and signaled for him to fly higher while I dangled from his tail for the thrill of it. He playfully threw me onto the snowy mountain and I gasped at the cold, he landed no bigger than a hatchling right next to me in an explosion of snow. We played for a minute before we started circling the large crater of the ancient volcano to find any signs of dragon life. I was about ready to give up when what seemed to be an elf jumped out of the snow and pinned me down, “Foresworn!” He bellowed.

A knife was to my throat, “Eka aí fricai un Shur’tugal!” I shouted, “I am a rider and friend!”

He froze and shuffled off of me quickly. Aden came to me and slowly grew to the size of a small horse. “What are you doing here?” He demanded in the Ancient Language.

“I am of the Dragonheart family Elda. I have come to see if any eggs have made it through the war at long last.” I said in the same tongue.

He was old, even by elfin standards. He looked likely to be in his late forties, which meant he was much older than when Galbatorix started his war against the riders, collecting dragon hearts as he went. His almond eyes squinted at me untrustworthily, “I must challenge you, Shur’tugal. For I know that Galbatorix has two eggs in his possession, you very well might be here on his orders.”

I nodded, no sooner had I dropped my guard did he press into my mind. Going through everything he would need to make sure I was not a threat, “You run from the Shadeslayer, why?” He demanded.

“If he were to catch me I would have to swear fealty to the Varden, or with the elves.” I said evenly. “Are you satisfied?”

A grin broke out on his old face, “Yes Shur’tugal Eden, and I have four here for you.” He said, diving back into the snow. _Four_, I couldn’t breath, I felt my dragon heart warm and throb in excitement.

I was still struggling to gain composure when he returned with two eggs under each arm, Aden collapsed next to me in surprise and disbelief. One was a lot like his own egg had been, pure white but with veins that were blacker than a starless night instead of the reflective blue seen in moonstone. Next to it was a deep green egg that had deep brown veins, like the bark of the trees in the Du Weldenvarden. In his other arm was a sky blue egg with deep blue veins, like the ocean on a bright day. Next to it was a purple one that’s coloration was deeper than the dwarven clan Az Sweldn rak Anhûin’s precious amethyst had ever gotten with pure white veins that were like the first egg.

He set them in the powdery snow in front of us, and then he pulled two shining stones from his pocket and showed them to us. It was the dragon hearts of their sire and mother no doubt, “They gave me these to give to the rider who was more dragon than rider, I know that is you because I can sense your dragon heart beating loud and true next to your human and elf one.” He said in the ancient language. Aden stiffened at the mention of his rider’s weakness, “Do not worry Skulblaka, your rider’s existence is an ancient and well-guarded secret of all who swore to protect the dragon eggs he, or in this case she, is fated to collect.” He said in human tongue but using the Ancient Word for dragon.

I took the hearts from him and put them in my pouch with the trinkets I made, he conjured pouches from the dwarf city far below and put two of each eggs in them before putting the two pouches in a satchel. “Thank you Elda, may the riders of old guide you back to your home safely.” I said, giving him the elfin blessing of a safe journey.

He put his fingers to his lips and greeted me with parting, “I will return to Ellesméra, and forge you a Rider’s fangs.” He vowed.

“There is no more brightsteel in the word Elda, how do you propose to forge me a rider’s weapon?” I said sadly, remembering my dream as the elf Rhunön and Eragon on making him his sword Brisinger. She had said there was enough ore to make several swords but she had hidden it away and locked her memory of its location.

He waved his hand out to the mountain peaks around us, “There is brightsteel in the mountain tops Fireraiser, I will collect enough of it to make many a sword for the rebirth of the riders.” He said. Something sparked as he caught sight of the rainbows dancing off Aden's scales. Addressing Aden he said, “Great Moonscales, in order to create the weapons your rider will need I must take some of your scales to serve as coal for the fire they will be born from.”

After he stared at him with unblinking ghost-blue eyes for what would seem like eternity to the elf he took a deep breath and shook himself, a shower of transparent white and iridescent blue scales falling from him that were quickly replace with fresh ones on his skin. _Take them then, Elda, for my rider has gone too long relying on magic alone._ He said.

The elf waved his hand passively and the scales blew toward him and into another pack he conjured like miniature vortexes of mystical color, “When you come to Ellesméra with your brood I shall have your fangs made and ready for you.” He said, putting his finger to his lips and disappearing before our eyes.

I looked around in awe, not noticing as Aden picked up the pack and brought it to me. I snapped out of it and carefully strapped the eggs to my back, feeling the heavy burden of motherhood before me. Aden scooped me onto his head and I slid down his smooth neck to the ridge I was meant to sit in and took off for the next mountain that we would check for eggs.
