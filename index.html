Welcome to my Little Corner!

Come in, get comfortable, and stay awhile.

I'm a mother and spouse with a creative heart, and someone who doesn't run out of ideas easily. Between family, learning new things, and finding time for the projects that keep my imagination busy, there's usually a little something happening wherever life takes me.

I enjoy getting lost in stories, bringing ideas to life through writing and handmade art, and challenging myself to learn things I never thought I'd try. Lately, that has meant exploring the world of web development and finding new ways to combine creativity with technology.

This little corner is a collection of the things that make me, well, me! You'll find the stories I write, the things I create, the ideas I'm exploring, and the little pieces of life I feel like sharing.

So take a look around, there's pleanty to explore!
